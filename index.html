Privacy

Clear about where your photos go

Here's the short version. The full privacy policy has every detail.

AI portraits

  • Nothing is uploaded until you give explicit consent.
  • Your two selected parent photos and the portrait prompt are sent over HTTPS through BabyLook's Oracle-hosted server in Frankfurt to Snapgen (Nano Banana 2 model) to create the image.
  • Photos go to Snapgen only to create your image. BabyLook doesn't use them for AI training and doesn't keep them on its servers after the request. Snapgen's handling is described in its own privacy policy.
  • You can revoke consent in Settings at any time. That stops future uploads; it does not erase data providers already hold.

Everything else

  • Baby Journal data (baby profile, memories, photos, reminder times) stays on your device and is not sent to the AI provider.
  • Generated portraits, keepsakes and settings are stored on your device.
  • No advertising SDK, no login and no cloud photo sync.
  • Apple and RevenueCat process purchase and subscription information.
  • If you report a generated image, the report (reason, optional note, task and account identifiers) is stored on BabyLook's server for safety review. The image itself is not uploaded.
FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it a genetic prediction of what my baby will look like?

No. BabyLook creates artistic AI interpretations inspired by two parent photos. They are fictional portraits made for fun and keepsakes, not genetic or medical predictions. The blend slider is a creative preference, and resemblance may vary.

What happens to my photos?

Only after your explicit consent, the two parent photos you selected and the portrait prompt are sent over HTTPS through BabyLook's server in Frankfurt to Snapgen, the AI image provider. They are used only to create your image; BabyLook doesn't use them for AI training or keep them on its servers after the request, and Snapgen's own privacy policy describes how it handles data. Baby Journal photos stay on your device unless you save or share them yourself.

Please read the privacy policy before uploading, and only use photos you have permission to use.

Is the first portrait really free?

Yes. Your first baby portrait is free without a subscription: one Classic portrait at standard resolution with a watermark. It's a one-time allowance.

What does Premium include?

BabyLook Pro ($0.99/week or $24.99/year in the US) includes up to 5 portraits per rolling week, the Cozy knits, Birthday, Garden and Storybook themes, growing-up portraits at ages 3, 5 and 10, four-portrait collections (4 credits), watermark-free HD images, personalized keepsakes, and full Baby Journal access with unlimited memories and every premium sticker, layout, background, font and filter.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose BabyLook. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login and no cloud photo sync. Your portraits and journal live on your device.
