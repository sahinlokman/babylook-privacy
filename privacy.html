BabyLook Privacy Policy

Policy text updated 15 October 2026; Baby Journal section added 7 November 2026. This document covers BabyLook only.
Contact: lokman782782@gmail.com

This page mirrors the policy linked from the app, with an added Baby Journal section describing on-device journal data. The canonical version is published at appterms.site/babylook/privacy/; if the two ever differ, the canonical version applies.

Photos and AI processing

BabyLook sends the two selected reference photos (which may contain face data) and the requested portrait prompt through its Oracle-hosted backend in Frankfurt to Snapgen, using Nano Banana 2. Growing-up portraits use the revealed child portrait as reference. Processing occurs only after your explicit consent. The result is an artistic simulation, not a genetic or medical prediction.

Snapgen may retain reference images, prompts, generation history and output images. Its exact retention period and provider-wide no-training commitment have not been verified. Immediate deletion is not guaranteed. Revoking consent stops future uploads; it does not erase data already held by providers.

Local data

Chosen photos are kept in app memory for the current flow. Generated portraits, keepsakes, settings and usage information are stored on your device. You may delete photographs/history and revoke processing consent in Settings. Keepsake names and notes are rendered locally and are not sent to the AI provider. Saving or sharing exports the selected image to the destination you choose.

Baby Journal

Baby Journal data, including your baby's profile (name, due or birth date and gender choice), memories, journal photos and reminder times, stays on your device. It is not uploaded to BabyLook's backend and is not sent to the AI provider. Journal reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. Saving or sharing a memory exports it to the destination you choose.

Usage and subscriptions

RevenueCat and Apple App Store or Google Play process installation identifiers, purchase and subscription information to provide billing and restore. The backend verifies subscriptions before paid generation. On iOS, Apple-signed AppTransaction and transaction proofs establish access; raw account identifiers are hashed for quota records. On Android, Play Integrity evaluates app/device/licensing integrity. An anonymous server-generated installation ID and secure session establish access; the backend uses RevenueCat to verify Google Play entitlement. Usage markers are kept without an automatic expiry to enforce one free portrait; paid generation timestamps use a rolling seven-day quota. Operational logs may contain IP/network metadata and request/error timestamps; BabyLook application logs do not include selected photos or session secrets.

No login or cloud photo synchronization

This version does not provide a shared account or history across iOS and Android. Android free allowance is installation-based and may reset on reinstall. No advertising SDK is integrated in this application.

Your choices

Use only photos you have permission to process. Do not upload someone else's photos without consent. Contact us for privacy questions or requests. Requests affecting provider-held data are subject to the provider's capabilities; deletion of remote data is not promised by deleting local history.
